A simple study plan

  1. Get the big picture. Open LAMP Flow, click through the six stages, then step through the main thread.
  2. Learn one part at a time. Press Part I at the top, then open each chapter and its concepts on the Mind Map.
  3. Recall it. Run a Flashcards deck for that part. Mark "Got it" only if you remembered without peeking.
  4. Test it. Take a Quiz on the part, then open the concepts you missed from the results.
  5. Repeat for Parts II to IV, then play the Drills for structure and templates.
  6. Apply it. Run the Simulation, then fill in a Gold Sheet for one of your real accounts.

How progress works

Every concept has a ring that fills from 0 to 3.

  • A correct answer adds a step. A wrong answer removes one.
  • A full ring means mastered. Chapter and part rings show the average of their concepts.
  • The Part buttons at the top narrow the Mind Map, Flashcards, Quiz and Drills to one part.
  • Reset progress, at the top, clears every ring.
